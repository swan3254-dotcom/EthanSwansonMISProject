Why I Chose UMD

I chose the University of Minnesota Duluth (UMD) because of its strong academic programs, welcoming campus community, and the opportunities it offers for personal and professional growth. UMD's focus on student success and its supportive environment made it the perfect place for me to pursue my major in Financial Planning.

What I Like About Duluth

Duluth is an amazing city with a unique blend of natural beauty and vibrant culture. I love the access to Lake Superior, the scenic hiking trails, and the friendly atmosphere. The surrounding area offers endless opportunities for outdoor activities, from exploring parks to enjoying the changing seasons. Duluth truly feels like a place where you can learn, grow, and have fun all at once.
