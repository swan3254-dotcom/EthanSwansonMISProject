Discover UMD and Duluth

Welcome to my Discover page! Here, I'll share insights about why I chose the University of Minnesota Duluth, what makes Duluth a special place, and my experiences as a student. Explore the unique blend of academic excellence and outdoor adventure that makes UMD an incredible place to study.

UMD Introduction Video (30 seconds)

Why I Chose UMD

I chose UMD because of its strong academic programs in Financial Planning, the welcoming campus community, and the incredible opportunities for personal growth. UMD combines affordable tuition with top-tier education and research, making it an ideal environment for ambitious students.

The faculty are supportive, the campus facilities are modern, and the location along Lake Superior offers a unique setting for learning. As a Bulldog, I'm proud to be part of this vibrant community that values both academic excellence and work-life balance.

What I Love About Duluth

Duluth is a city rich in natural beauty and cultural vibrancy. Nestled at the western tip of Lake Superior, it offers breathtaking views and year-round outdoor activities. The Aerial Lift Bridge, Glensheen Historic Estate, and the many parks make it a picturesque place to live and study.

What I love most about Duluth is the perfect balance between urban convenience and nature. During the winter, cross-country skiing and ice fishing are popular, while summer brings sailing, hiking, and festivals. The community is friendly and involved, creating a warm atmosphere for students from all over.

My UMD Experiences

Studying at UMD has been an incredible journey. I've had the opportunity to participate in research projects, join student organizations like the Baseball Team, and engage in community service. The small class sizes allow for personalized attention from professors, and the interdisciplinary nature of programs helps build well-rounded skills.

Whether it's attending Bulldogs games, exploring the North Shore, or connecting with other students, every day at UMD brings new discoveries and growth opportunities.
